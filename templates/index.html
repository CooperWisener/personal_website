About Me

Hi, I'm Cooper — a Data Science student at the University of Wisconsin–Madison (graduating May 2027) with a minor in Business and a 3.84 GPA. I'm passionate about applying statistical modeling and machine learning to real-world problems, with a particular interest in sports analytics and psychology.

I currently work as an Associate Consultant at Krahnborn, where I help client organizations get the most out of Salesforce — configuring objects, page layouts, and user permissions to fit how each business actually operates, and building the declarative automations, reports, and dashboards that sit on top of them. When configuration alone isn't enough, I write custom Lightning Web Components in JavaScript and HTML to extend the platform. A lot of the job is working directly with consultants and client stakeholders to gather requirements and translate them into solution designs, then validating the data across sandbox and production before anything ships.

I formerly worked as a Data Science Research Assistant at the Niedenthal Emotions Lab at UW–Madison, where I applied NLP techniques to analyze emotion-related datasets and support ongoing psychological research. Paper coming soon!

Outside the lab, I've built projects including NFL analytics models using Markov chains and XGBoost, a data dashboard visualizing data collected from a Google Form, and a full-stack web app powered by the Spotify and ChatGPT APIs. I'm always looking for new problems to tackle with data.
